# PocketSmart AI — Delivery Outcomes

- [ ] **Responsive marketing experience:** Provide a welcoming landing page with a product explanation, planner selection for Home Interior, Party Budget, and Jewelry, testimonials, and footer; make the experience responsive and usable on mobile.
- [ ] **Authentication surfaces:** Provide registration and login screens with validated inputs and clear success, error, and loading feedback; use the managed Manus OAuth session flow rather than inventing a local user.
- [ ] **Personalized dashboard:** Provide a dashboard showing recent recommendations, saved queries, tailored budget insights, and recommendation history; show a clear logged-out state when no application session is available.
- [ ] **Home Interior Planner:** Accept total budget, room details, and item quantities across lighting, fans, dining, furniture, and decor; produce balanced marketplace-inspired recommendations with budget allocation.
- [ ] **Party Budget Planner:** Accept budget, guest count, event type, venue details, and preferences; allocate funds across catering, decoration, entertainment, and accommodation with vendor-inspired suggestions.
- [ ] **Jewelry Planner:** Accept budget, occasion, style, color, and material preferences, plus an optional outfit image upload for occasion-aware matching suggestions.
- [ ] **Structured recommendation results:** Display reusable cards with item or service name, platform, price, rationale, budget fit, and relevant links or actions.
- [ ] **Recommendation management:** Let users save queries or results, revisit history, and navigate between planners and prior recommendations.
- [ ] **Complete UX states:** Include form validation, loading indicators, empty states, responsive controls, accessible feedback, and mobile-friendly layouts across the application.
- [ ] **Modular fallback engine:** Provide realistic deterministic demo recommendations and clean service boundaries ready for future Gemini multimodal and third-party marketplace or vendor API integration.
